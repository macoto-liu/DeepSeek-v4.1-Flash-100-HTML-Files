The exposure triangle

Three apertures onto the same light

01

The iris

Aperture is the size of the hole the light falls through. Wide open at f/1.4, the dusk pours in and the world softens at its edges. Closed to f/16, it admits a whisper and holds everything sharp.

f/1.4 — f/16 · seven blades · half-stop click
02

The blink

Shutter speed is how long the dark is allowed in. A thousandth of a second freezes a swallow mid-turn; a full second lets the river forget where its edge was.

1/2000 — 1″ · focal-plane · one curtain
03

The ear

ISO is the sensitivity of the film to what little light remains. Raise it and the scene arrives sooner, carrying grain like a voice on an old recording. Lower it and wait.

100 — 6400 · native 200 · monochrome grain
