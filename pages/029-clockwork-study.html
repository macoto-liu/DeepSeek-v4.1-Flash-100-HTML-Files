A study in mechanical time

A machine
for noticing.

going train · three wheels chapter ring · 60 divisions
Fig. 1 — going train Poinçon de maître · C.07 Montre → —

A mechanical watch does not measure time; it negotiates with it. Energy leaves a coiled spring in fractions too small to feel, and a train of wheels turns that trickle into a regular argument of seconds.

The escapement is the hinge of the whole affair — a tiny pallet that locks, releases, locks again, twenty-one thousand six hundred times an hour. Between each beat the hands hold still. That stillness is not a flaw. It is the smallest unit of attention the machine can offer.

Wind the mainspring and watch the train quicken. Stop the escapement and the conversation pauses, though the afternoon outside carries on.
