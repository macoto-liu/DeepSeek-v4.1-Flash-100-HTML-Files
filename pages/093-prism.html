On the decomposition of light

White is a crowd.

A white beam is not one colour but many, travelling together at the same speed. Set a glass prism in its path and the crowd separates — each wavelength bending by its own degree and fanning into a spectrum. Drag the prism, tilt it, and pull the dispersion apart.
