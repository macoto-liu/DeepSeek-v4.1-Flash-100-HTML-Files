Words by an unnamed hand / Set in grotesque & serif

IA Small Weather01 / 06

Morning arrives

without a forecast.

We dress inside it.

A low sky, held in the mouth —
the taste of tin and green.

Low pressure / 1,012 hPa“the day has not decided”Wind SW, 4 knots
IIBarometer02 / 06

The barometer

leans toward

the window.

Somewhere, rain is deciding.
Pressure is only a way of listening.

Falling slowly“a needle, a held breath”28.94 in
IIIWindow03 / 06

A circle of glass

keeps the weather

at arm’s length.

Outside, the town is slightly out of focus.
We call this distance a room.

Visibility 8 km“the glass keeps its own small climate”West-facing
IVRain04 / 06

Rain writes

its thin alphabet

on the awning,

on the idea of leaving.

Every drop a small correction.
The street edits itself.

4 mm / hour“nothing falls in a straight line”Umbrella, unopened
VAfter05 / 06

After the storm,

the air is

a blue room.

We stand in it without speaking.
The weather has no memory, but we do.

Clearing / 17:42“the furniture of light, rearranged”Wind NW, 2 knots
VIForecast06 / 06

Tomorrow:

small weather,

occasional light,

a chance of staying.

Bring nothing. We are already here.
The forecast is a kind of promise.

Outlook / fair“end of poem, beginning of weather”06 / 06

An original poem in six movements, composed for the space between a window and a person. Set in a neo-grotesque for the large lines and an italic serif for the quieter ones; printed on warm cream, with vermilion and petrol blue.
