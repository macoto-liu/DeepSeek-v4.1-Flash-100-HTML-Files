The alphabet as botany

Letters,pressed.

Every alphabet is a herbarium. A letter is gathered young — a curve, a stem, a serif like a small leaf — then laid between the pages of a dictionary until it holds its shape. These twenty-six specimens were not printed; they were grown. Each arrangement is raised from the character code of its letter, so that A is never B, and no pressing is quite repeated. Read the plate as a page from a field notebook: the Latin name is invented, the locality is approximate, and the flower is always in season. Press one, and the book keeps it.
