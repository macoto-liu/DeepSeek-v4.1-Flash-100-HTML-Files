The architecture
of a letter.

Every decision is structural. MONOLITH is drawn on a 1000-unit em with a generous x-height, blunt terminals and counters that hold their shape at every size.

Nothing decorative.
Everything deliberate.

The counters are drawn first and the mass is fitted around them. A wide, square shoulder gives the face its catalogue presence; flat terminals keep the page quiet.
