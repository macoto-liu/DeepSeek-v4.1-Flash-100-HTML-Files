Light
Bright, indirect. A north window is generous; afternoon sun is not.
Water
Mist twice a week, lid closed. If the glass clears entirely, mist once more.
Air
Open the lid for ten minutes each month. Let the old air leave; let the room in.
Pruning
Pinch a frond if it crowds the glass. It will answer with two more.
Paper · recycled · keep dryGrow slowly.

Planted, not manufactured.

Each vessel is blown in a small studio outside Rotterdam, then planted the same week so the roots never know a shelf. The sand is from the Maas; the stone is from a quarry we visit twice a year; the moss arrives on the soles of our boots.
