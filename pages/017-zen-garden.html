Karesansui is a Japanese dry landscape garden. Raked sand suggests water; stones suggest mountains. Drag a finger or pointer over the sand to carve five parallel grooves, or use the rake anew button to smooth the garden.

Karesansui — the dry landscape garden. Gravel is raked into water; a few stones become mountains; moss holds the edge of the world. Nothing is planted, and nothing is finished. The rake is the only weather.
