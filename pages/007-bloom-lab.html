Field notes Three small truths
I.

On soil

Nothing is planted in a straight line. The field keeps its own survey, and the best stems arrive slightly out of register.

II.

On light

Specimens are drawn, not photographed. Every edge is a decision; every shadow is a little more paper.

III.

On patience

A bloom takes longer than a click. Stay for the opening. The field will still be here when you look away.
