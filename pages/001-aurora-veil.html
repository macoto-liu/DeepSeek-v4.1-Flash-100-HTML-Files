The studio

We do not design light. We give it a room.

Practice · 2019—

Founded under the polar night, Aurora Veil moves between optical research and spatial installation. Work is made slowly, in small editions, with the weather as a collaborator.

Commissions begin with a season, a window and a question about how a room should feel at dusk.
