Echoes / no. 05
01
Room oneThe threshold

Halli

The door still holds the shape of a hand that never knocked. Dust settles in the hallway like an answer rehearsed for years. You step in; the floorboards recognize your weight. A coat hangs on its hook, though no one has worn it since the winter the clocks stopped.

Marginal note

The hall clock was sold in 1998. Its tick stayed.

I / VHall
02
Room twoThe kettle

Kitchenii

The kettle keeps a small, patient grudge. On the sill, three jars of light — preserves made from a summer no one names. The table is set for the number of people we used to be. At dusk the window gives the room back to itself, and for a moment it is exactly enough.

Marginal note

Recipe for a room: one window, two chairs, a door left ajar.

II / VKitchen
03
Room threeThirteen steps

Stairiii

Thirteen steps. Count them going up and you will always arrive at fourteen. The banister is warm where the house holds on. Somewhere between the seventh and eighth, a voice clears its throat and decides against speaking. Light falls from the landing as though it has forgotten what it came for.

Marginal note

We painted over the pencil marks. They grew anyway.

III / VStair
04
Room fourUnder the roof

Atticiv

Here the roof remembers rain before it falls. Boxes labelled with years we cannot open. A dress, still holding the posture of standing. Light arrives through one small window, late and apologetic, and finds the dust arranged like a sentence nobody finished.

Marginal note

The attic is where the house keeps its second heart.

IV / VAttic
05
Room fiveBeyond the glass

Gardenv

Behind the glass, the garden continues without us. Roses revise themselves each June. The path ends at a gate with no fence, which is how the house explains the world. You open the door; the air smells of rain that has not arrived. The house exhales, and lets you go.

Marginal note

What grows back is never the same garden. It is the same ground.

V / VGarden

The house keeps the rest.
