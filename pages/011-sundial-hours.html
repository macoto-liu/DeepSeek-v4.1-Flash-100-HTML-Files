Field notes / I—IIIOn the reading of shadowsFirenze, MMXXVI
01GNOMON

The blade

A triangular gnomon stands at the centre. Its edge is cut to the latitude of Florence, 43°46′ north; the steel is the instrument, and everything else is only shadow. At apparent noon the shadow lies shortest, pointing true north.

02EQUATION

The sun is not exact

Our star runs fast in November, slow in February, by as much as sixteen minutes. This difference is the equation of time. The dial reports it plainly and refuses to correct it — a clock should admit its weather.

03HOUR

The older account

Solar time begins at noon, not midnight. Sunrise and sunset move through the year, and daylight grows or withdraws at both ends. To read the dial is to accept the day as it is given, not as it is scheduled.
