The form

The cento is an old machine made of other people's parts: lines lifted whole from their poems and set beside strangers until a new argument appears. It is composition by arrangement — the opposite of theft, or its most honest form.

This edition

One hundred fragments were written for this page and released into a 10 × 10 index. Every line can be taken once. The poem you are making exists only in this browser, in this order, for as long as the page is open.

The making

Set in a public serif on warm ivory, with oxblood for the borrowed and antique gold for the index. Built as a single HTML page, without images, networks, or saved state. Everything borrowed. Nothing lost.
