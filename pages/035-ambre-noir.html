The composition

Top · Heart · Base
Extrait — 28%

TopI

The first light

  • Bergamot
  • Pink pepper
  • Bitter orange

A cold spark at the opening: citrus peel torn over black pepper, gone almost before it arrives.

HeartII

Powder & shadow

  • Iris absolute
  • Orris root
  • Jasmine

The room turns inward. Iris gives the scent its pale, suede softness; jasmine keeps it alive.

BaseIII

The long exhale

  • Amber
  • Labdanum
  • Vetiver
  • Tonka

Resin, smoke and warm skin. The drydown stays close, like a coat carried in from the cold.

IVNotes from
the atelier
“I wanted a fragrance that behaves like a room at dusk — the last light on the wall, the smell of resin warming in a closed hand.”

Ambre Noir was built around a single accord of labdanum and orris, left to macerate for seven weeks before the amber was added. Nothing in it is bright for long.

Élodie Vannier
