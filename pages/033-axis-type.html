On the variable/ 01

A letter used to be a fixed object: cut in metal, then in film, then in pixels. The variable font loosens that certainty. One file now carries a whole family — a hairline and a black, a whisper and a shout — and the designer is invited to move between them.

AXIS treats that motion as material. Push a slider and the letterform answers immediately: weight, width, slant and optical size are not presets but coordinates. What you are looking at is not a picture of type. It is type, being worked.
