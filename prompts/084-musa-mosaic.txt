Create a tactile mosaic studio called "MUSA". Warm stone background, a framed mosaic panel of hundreds of tiny tessera tiles, and a narrow atelier control rail with six stone colours (terracotta, ochre, sea, ivory, black, olive), three pattern buttons (Medallion, Wave, Scatter), a Clear control and a placed-tile counter. The mosaic is a 24×16 grid with visible grout, slightly irregular tiles and a classical rosette generated on load. Visitors paint by clicking or dragging across the panel; pattern buttons regenerate a symmetrical medallion or wave composition; Clear empties the grout. Include a museum plaque, a gold double frame and a footer. Fully responsive, one standalone HTML file with inline CSS/JS, no external images or fonts.
