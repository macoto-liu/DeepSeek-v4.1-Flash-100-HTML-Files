Create an interactive 3D flip book telling a short illustrated story called "The Cartographer's Sleep". Warm paper, ink blue and vermilion; a book on a table with a visible spine, page edges and a soft shadow. The left page carries the title and a short introduction; the right side is a stack of three turning sheets with stories and simple SVG illustrations — a map, a window, a sleeping figure and a small boat. Clicking the page corner or the Next button turns a sheet in 3D; Previous turns it back. Page numbers, a progress line and a "close the book" reset complete the interface. Fully responsive, one standalone HTML file with inline CSS/JS/SVG, no external images or fonts.
