Create an interactive isometric city called "Isoline". A warm peach-to-cream sky, soft clouds and birds, and a detailed isometric city drawn in SVG: eight-by-eight blocks, pastel buildings with three visible faces, tiny windows, trees, shadows and paved lots. Visitors can click any lot to add or raise a building; "New city" regenerates the whole town with a new palette and skyline; a Day / Night toggle lights the windows and deepens the sky. A large editorial headline "A city, one block at a time." sits over the top left, with a small control bar and building count bottom right. Fully responsive, one standalone HTML file with inline CSS/JS/SVG, no external images or fonts.
