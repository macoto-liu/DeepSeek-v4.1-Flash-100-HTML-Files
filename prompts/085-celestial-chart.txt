Create an elegant natal-chart page called "Celestial Chart". Warm laid paper, oxblood and brass engraved lines, with a large circular astrology wheel on the left: zodiac band with glyphs, twelve house cusps, ten planets placed at their degrees, and coloured aspect lines across the centre. The right column holds the title "The sky you were born under.", the birth data, a list of planets with glyphs and degrees, and a toggle for aspect lines. Hovering or selecting a planet highlights it and updates a short interpretation card; the chart is fixed to 17 October 2026, 14:20, Helsinki. Fully responsive, one standalone HTML file with inline CSS/JS/SVG, no external images or fonts.
