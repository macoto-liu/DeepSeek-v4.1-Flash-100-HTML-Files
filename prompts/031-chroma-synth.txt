Create a playable retro-futurist synthesizer called CHROMA-4 inside a luxurious hardware panel. Dark charcoal and brushed graphite, warm ivory labels, and twelve luminous pads in coral, amber, mint, cyan, violet and rose. The layout: a logo header with power LED, a large oscilloscope canvas, three slim hardware faders (cutoff, resonance, tempo), a 4×3 pad grid with note names and keyboard hints, a sixteen-step sequencer strip, transport controls, and small engraved serial numbers. Clicking a pad plays a real synthesized note via WebAudio, lights the pad and animates the scope; the sequencer can run patterns at variable tempo, with a moving playhead. Everything must be usable and beautiful on mobile. Fully responsive, one standalone HTML file with inline CSS/JS, no external libraries, fonts or audio files.
