Create a botanical clock called "Floral Time". A warm paper background, sage green, terracotta, gold and rose; the aesthetic of an antique herbarium crossed with a modern wall clock. A large circular dial contains twelve flowers, one for each hour, arranged around a ring of stems and leaves. The current hour's flower is fully open; the others remain closed buds. A slender second hand shaped like a leaf sweeps the dial, and the hour and minute hands are drawn as stems. The left column carries the title "The hour, in bloom.", a short note, a selected-flower card with name and meaning, and two buttons: "Advance an hour" and "Return to now". Hovering or clicking a flower previews it. Fully responsive, one standalone HTML file with inline CSS/JS/SVG, no external assets.
