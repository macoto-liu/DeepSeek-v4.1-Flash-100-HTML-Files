Create a full-screen generative sound sculpture called "Resonance". A near-black teal space, concentric rings of cyan, violet and magenta expanding from the pointer, a faint central waveform, and huge editorial type at the edges: "Everything is a wave." The interface is minimal — a small masthead, a bottom control tray with an "Emit" button, a frequency slider, a sound-on toggle and a live readout of hertz and amplitude. Clicking or tapping emits a visible ring and, only if sound is enabled, a soft synthesized tone. Rings drift, interfere and fade like ripples on dark water. The page should feel like an instrument for looking at sound. Fully responsive, one standalone HTML file with inline CSS/JS, no external audio or images.
