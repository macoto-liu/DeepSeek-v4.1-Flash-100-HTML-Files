Create a planet-generation laboratory called "World Foundry". Deep space black with a soft nebula glow, a large procedurally drawn planet on the right, and a narrow control column on the left. Four sliders control radius, water, cloud cover and temperature; four presets (Ocean, Desert, Ice, Gas) apply curated combinations; "Forge a new world" randomises and generates a new name and seed. The planet is drawn on canvas with bands, continents, cloud swirls, polar caps, a terminator shadow and an atmosphere rim, plus one small moon on an orbit. Readouts show radius, water, cloud, mean temperature, gravity and a generated designation. Fully responsive, one standalone HTML file with inline CSS/JS, no external images or fonts.
