Design "Bauhaus Spiel", an interactive geometric composition playground in the spirit of the Bauhaus poster tradition. Paper cream, hard black ink, and the three canonical primaries — red, blue and yellow — applied as flat, overprinted shapes. The page is arranged like a studio workbench: a compact masthead, a left tool rail for choosing circle / square / triangle / rule and selecting one of the four colours, and a large stage with a curated initial composition and oversized typography. Visitors click the stage to stamp a new shape, drag any shape to reposition it, and double-click one to remove it. Overlapping shapes use multiply blending to feel printed. Include a "recompose" button that rearranges the curated layout, a live object count, and a short manifesto footer. Fully responsive, one standalone HTML file with inline CSS/JS, no external assets.
