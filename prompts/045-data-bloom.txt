Create an editorial data-art page called "A Year in Petals" that charts a fictional year of weather as twelve radial flower diagrams. Deep ink-green background, warm cream text, chartreuse, coral and pale sky-blue. A left column holds the title "A year, in petals.", a short explanation and a selected-month panel with rainfall, sunshine, mean temperature and a one-line seasonal note. The right side is a 4×3 grid of flowers, one per month; each flower has twelve petals whose lengths encode weekly sunshine and whose colours shift with rainfall. A Sunshine / Rainfall toggle remaps the petal lengths, hovering enlarges a flower, and clicking selects it and updates the panel. Fully responsive, one standalone HTML file with inline CSS/JS/SVG, no external libraries or fonts.
