Create a full-screen interactive light painting called "Aura". A near-black space holds soft additive plumes of colour that bloom from the cursor as it moves, drifting and fading like smoke seen through a lens. Three palettes — Aurora (teal, violet, rose), Ember (amber, coral, red) and Tidal (blue, aqua, white) — can be switched live. The interface is minimal and editorial: a small masthead, one oversized serif statement "trace the air." in the lower left, a palette switch and clear control in the lower right, and faint field notes at the edges. On load a gentle pre-drawn plume should make the screen feel inhabited. Add a subtle grain and vignette so the colour feels photographic. Fully responsive, one standalone HTML file with inline CSS/JS, no external assets.
